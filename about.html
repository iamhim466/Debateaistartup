Why we exist

Speech and debate reward preparation. But the things that make preparation possible are not shared equally.

A practice partner who argues the hard side. A judge who explains the ballot. Someone to help find the evidence. Some programs have all of that. Many do not, and a student's results often follow their access rather than their effort.

Flowed is built to close that gap. Any competitor can spar, get specific feedback and build a cited case at any hour, in any event, from interpretation and oratory to Congress and policy debate. Coaches get a clear view of their whole team, so their time goes to coaching.

What we believe

  1. 01

    Every event belongs.

    Speech and debate are one community. We build for every format, and no event is an afterthought.

  2. 02

    Evidence stays honest.

    A card is quoted word for word, cited in full and linked to its source. Nothing is reworded to fit an argument.

  3. 03

    Feedback is specific.

    A score means little without a reason. Every note ties back to something you actually said.

  4. 04

    Coaches stay in charge.

    The AI handles repetition. People decide what a student needs and when to step in.

Our promise

What you can count on, and what you never will.

We will

  • Quote every source exactly, with a link back
  • Explain how each score was reached
  • Keep coaches informed and in control
  • Work for every speech and debate event

We won't

  • Rewrite or paraphrase evidence
  • Pretend to replace your coach
  • Hide the reasoning behind a ballot
  • Treat any event as second-class

Every speaker deserves a fair shot at the final round.
