01 · Briefs

Briefs built like the ones champions carry.

Name a topic and Flowed assembles the full case file: the framing, both sides, and the answers to the attacks you'll face.

  • Topic analysis and sourced definitions.
  • Pro and con contentions, each with claim, warrant and impact.
  • Frontlines and blocks for the arguments you'll hear most.
  • Evidence cards slotted under the argument they support.

02 · Evidence

Cited. Verbatim. Unchanged.

Every card quotes its source word for word. Flowed never rewrites, trims into a new meaning, or paraphrases evidence, so what you read is what the author wrote.

  • Full citation: author, credentials, publication and date.
  • The exact lines worth reading aloud are highlighted.
  • A link back to the original so any judge can check it.
